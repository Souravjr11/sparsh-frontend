FAQ

Usually we take an advance to confirm the booking, and the remaining amount on event day.

Cancellations depend on the date and preparation. Contact us early for the best possible resolution.

How long do bands perform?

Performance duration can be 1 hour, 2 hours, or full-event based on your package.

Do you provide travel / outstation bookings?

Yes. Travel and stay (if required) are discussed before confirmation.
