Our Heritage

The Story Behind EVENTIQUE Group of Events

EVENTIQUE was born out of a profound passion for the electrifying power of live sound and the sheer magic of unforgettable celebrations. What started in 2018 as a dedicated acoustic ensemble in Kolkata has evolved into an end-to-end event production enterprise trusted by hundreds of families, couples, and corporate leaders.

"We believe an event is not just a gathering — it is a symphony of sound, lights, emotion, and seamless execution that leaves an indelible mark on every guest."

From royal destination weddings in Rajasthan to high-octane college fests and intimate birthday soirées, our team manages every technical rider, acoustic decibel, and stage visual with surgical precision.

🎯 Our Mission

Deliver Zero-Stress Excellence

👁️ Our Vision

India's Benchmark for Live Events

To set the gold standard across India for live musical acts, bespoke themed weddings, and immersive festival experiences through creativity and technical innovation.
